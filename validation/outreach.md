Goal: confirm whether businesses already receiving x402/agent stablecoin payments actually
feel the "unreadable wall of micro-transactions" pain described in the brief, before building
categorization logic for a problem that might still be early.

## Outreach message (short, non-salesy)

Subject: Quick question about your x402 payment records

Hi — I saw [Company] accepts x402/stablecoin payments for [API/product]. I'm looking into the
accounting side of agent-to-agent payments (categorizing and reporting on the flood of small
automated transactions businesses receive) and wanted to ask a quick, no-pitch question:

How do you currently track and make sense of these payments for your own records — who paid,
how much, and how it rolls up for revenue/tax purposes? Is it something you've solved, something
you eyeball, or something you haven't gotten to yet?

Genuinely just trying to understand the real pain (or lack of it) before building anything. Happy
to share what I learn back with you if useful. Thanks for your time!

— [Your name]

## What to listen for

- "We don't, it's a mess" / "we just eyeball it" → strong validation, note their specific pain points.
- "We already solved it with X" → find out how, what X is, and what's still annoying about it.
- "We don't get enough volume for this to matter yet" → validation that the market is early, note
  approximate volume/frequency they mention.
